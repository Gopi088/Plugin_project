Position: Global Tax Specialist
Location: Pan India
Experience Required: 7–10 Years
Role Overview
We are looking for a Senior Tax Compliance professional with strong experience in
FATCA, CRS, and US tax regulatory reporting within a Banking or Financial Services
environment.
This role requires end-to-end ownership of global tax reporting processes, regulatory
interpretation, remediation initiatives, and stakeholder coordination across
Compliance, Operations, and Technology teams.
Key Responsibilities
• Lead FATCA / CRS reporting across multiple jurisdictions.
• Oversee W8 / W9 validation and withholding tax processes.
• Ensure compliance with IRS Chapter 3 & 4 regulations and manage regulatory
submissions.
• Drive remediation activities and regulatory change initiatives.
• Maintain audit readiness and strong governance standards.
• Collaborate with cross-functional teams to strengthen reporting controls.
• Support process optimization and documentation standardization.
Required Experience & Skills
• 7+ years of hands-on experience in FATCA / CRS / US Tax reporting within
Banking or Financial Services.
• Strong knowledge of withholding tax and regulatory compliance requirements.
• Experience managing remediation projects and regulatory updates.
• Exposure to multi-geography reporting environments.
• Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel for reporting and data analysis.
