1️⃣ Avaloq Experience – HARD GATE (DO NOT SKIP)
🗣️ Ask (verbatim)
“Have you worked on Avaloq Core Banking?
Please tell me about one Avaloq project and what you personally did in it.”
🔍 Probes (ask only if needed)
“Which Avaloq area was it – Wealth, portfolio, reporting, onboarding?”
“Who were the users – RM, operations, compliance?”
🎯 Expectation (depth signal)
Clearly says yes
Names specific Avaloq usage (not generic banking)
Explains own BA responsibility (not team work only)
⚠️ Grey / weak answers
“I was part of Avaloq program but not hands-on”
Very generic banking explanation
Cannot explain own role clearly
🧭 TA Tip
❌ If Avaloq experience is unclear / shaky → STOP & REJECT
Do not compensate with good BA answers later

2️⃣ Wealth Management Domain – REAL UNDERSTANDING
🗣️ Ask
“In Wealth Management, what kind of business work did you support on Avaloq?”
🔍 Probes
“Was it portfolio reports, transactions, fees, or client onboarding?”
“Who used the output?”
🎯 Expectation
Talks about client investments, portfolio value, reports, fees
Knows who uses what and why
Explains in simple business language
⚠️ Grey answers
“Wealth is about investments”
No mention of users or real business flow
🧭 TA Tip
Strong candidates talk business first, system second
If answer sounds like textbook → Grey

3️⃣ Business Requirement Clarity – THINKING DEPTH
🗣️ Ask
“When business asks for a new change in Avaloq, how do you make sure you fully understand what they want?”
🔍 Probes
“Do you ask questions or take requirement as is?”
“What do you do if requirement is not clear?”
🎯 Expectation
Mentions discussion, examples, follow-up questions
Talks about clarifying business rules
Mentions confirming back with business
⚠️ Grey answers
“Business sends mail, I convert to Jira”
No clarification step mentioned
🧭 TA Tip
We are testing thinking, not documentation
If they don’t ask questions → weak BA

4️⃣ From Requirement → Functional Spec (DEPTH CHECK)
🗣️ Ask
“After you understand the requirement, how do you write it down so developers don’t get confused?”
🔍 Probes
“Do you write rules or only high-level points?”
“Do developers come back with many questions?”
🎯 Expectation
Mentions business rules, conditions, exceptions
Talks about reducing rework
Shows ownership of clarity
⚠️ Grey answers
“I write high level, developers manage details”
Accepts confusion as normal
🧭 TA Tip
Senior BAs own clarity
If candidate avoids responsibility → downgrade

5️⃣ Handling Change & Ambiguity – MATURITY SIGNAL
🗣️ Ask
“If business changes the requirement mid-way, what do you usually do?”
🔍 Probes
“Do you push back or accept everything?”
“How do you explain impact?”
🎯 Expectation
Talks about impact analysis
Explains change to both business and IT
Mentions trade-offs
⚠️ Grey answers
“We just change it”
“Manager decides”
🧭 TA Tip
Look for judgement, not obedience

6️⃣ Working With Developers & QA – DELIVERY REALITY
🗣️ Ask
“How do you usually work with developers and testers on Avaloq projects?”
🔍 Probes
“Do you review before development?”
“Do you support during testing?”
🎯 Expectation
Talks about regular discussions
Helps clarify issues during build & test
Not a hand-off BA
⚠️ Grey answers
“After requirement, my job is done”
Very siloed approach
🧭 TA Tip
Avaloq projects need continuous BA involvement

7️⃣ Stakeholder Conflict – SENIORITY CHECK
🗣️ Ask
“Tell me about a time when business wanted something but IT said it’s not possible. What did you do?”
🔍 Probes
“How did you explain it to business?”
“What was the final decision?”
🎯 Expectation
Balanced explanation
Tries to find solution, not escalate immediately
Outcome focused
⚠️ Grey answers
“I escalated”
Blames business or IT
🧭 TA Tip
At M3/M4, BA is a bridge, not a messenger

8️⃣ Digital Transformation – JD ALIGNMENT
🗣️ Ask
“Have you worked on any system improvement or digital change in Wealth or Core Banking?”
🔍 Probes
“What changed for users?”
“What problem did it solve?”
🎯 Expectation
Talks about process improvement, automation, experience
Mentions Avaloq or wealth platform changes
⚠️ Grey answers
Just says “transformation project” with no detail
🧭 TA Tip
Transformation must show impact, not just name

9️⃣ Self-Reflection – AUTHENTICITY GATE
🗣️ Ask
“What part of your BA work are you strongest at, and what do you still find difficult?”
🎯 Expectation
Honest answer
Matches earlier discussion
⚠️ Red flags
“I’m strong in everything”
Contradictions
🧭 TA Tip
Over-confidence without reflection = risk

🧠 FINAL TA QUICK RULES (PIN THIS)
❌ No clear Avaloq → Reject
❌ Weak Wealth explanation → Grey / Reject
✅ Simple explanations + real examples → Strong
Depth > English > confidence
Use NA only if not asked
