✅ **TA-Friendly Role Summary
(AI/ML Architect — Manager + Senior Manager)**
(Updated & Unified for Tribera TAs)

Role:
AI/ML Architect
Levels:
Manager (10–15 YOE)
Senior Manager (15–20 YOE)
Industry: Media Preferred
Locations: Gurgaon / Pune / Bengaluru
Mode: Hybrid
Source JD: AI/ML Architect — Multimodal, LLM, Video AI, RAG, GPU Infra, Governance, Integrations, Strategy 

🎯 What This Role Is About (TA Pitch – 30 seconds)
This role is for enterprise-level AI/ML Architects who can design the full AI strategy and end-to-end architecture for a large media organization.
They build systems involving:
Multimodal AI: text + image + audio + video
LLMs + adapters + vector search
Video intelligence at scale (10,000+ hours of media)
Multimodal RAG (transcripts + frames + metadata)
GPU clusters + distributed compute
AI governance, auditability, lineage
CMS/MAM/data-lake/vector DB integrations
Manager (10–15 YOE)
Architects large systems, drives platform integration and design, ensures performance, scalability, and best practices.
Senior Manager (15–20 YOE)
Owns enterprise-wide AI strategy, governance, adoption of LLMOps, agentic automation, and C-level architectural communication.

🟦 Must-Have Skills (Manager vs Senior Manager)
Multimodal & Media AI

LLMs, RAG & Knowledge Systems

Platform & Infrastructure

Governance, Compliance & Security

Leadership & Innovation

🟩 Good-to-Have Skills
Experience with broadcast/OTT, MAM, DAM
Familiarity with video generation, audio synthesis, generative media
Experience designing AI COEs or platform orgs
Knowledge of NVIDIA Triton, Ray Serve, K8s for AI workloads
Understanding of content rights/IP protection workflows

🟥 Red Flags (Unified)
🔻 Multimodal / Media Gaps
Only NLP experience
Only CV classification/detection (no video/audio)
No exposure to large-scale media pipelines
🔻 LLM + RAG Weakness
Talks only about “using LangChain”
No knowledge of fine-tuning, adapters, or vector search
No understanding of retrieval quality, latency, or chunking
🔻 Infrastructure Gaps
No GPU optimization
No distributed compute experience
No cloud platform depth
🔻 Enterprise Architecture Gaps
Has executed but never architected
No system boundary design
No integrations with CMS/MAM/lakehouse
🔻 Governance / Compliance Weakness
No knowledge of data privacy, copyright/IP workflows
No experience with governance frameworks (NIST AI RMF)
🔻 Fakery Signals
Lists “CV + NLP + ASR + LLM + RAG + GPU + agentic” in one line
No examples, metrics, or outcomes
Copy-pasted responsibilities
Overpolished generic bullets like “architected AI platform end-to-end”

🟦 Behavioural Markers (Manager vs Senior Manager)
Manager — Strong Indicators
Talks about architectural decisions: “We chose X because…”
Describes real trade-offs (latency, cost, model size)
Clear ownership of large components
Pipeline-level reasoning (ingest → transform → infer → monitor)
Senior Manager — Strong Indicators
Talks at enterprise scale
Uses language like “roadmap”, “platform strategy”, “governance”, “lineage”
Handles ambiguity and stakeholder alignment
Describes cross-team orchestration and C-level communication
Shows maturity in designing compliant and secure systems

🟧 What Great Candidates Typically Say
Manager-Level Examples
“We processed 50K hours of content using a distributed ingestion pipeline with FFmpeg + Ray.”
“Our multimodal RAG pipeline combined transcript embeddings + frame embeddings using FAISS.”
“I designed the lineage tracking using MLflow + metadata store to meet audit requirements.”
Senior Manager-Level Examples
“I defined the AI architecture roadmap for multimodal LLMs across the org.”
“We created a governance framework aligned with NIST AI RMF.”
“We designed a GPU cluster with autoscaling policies for LLM workloads, reducing cost by X%.”
“I presented architecture trade-offs to C-level for our personalization platform.”
