📋 Tribera Resume Evaluation Framework
Version 2.1 | Internal Use Only

🔎 Executive Summary
Purpose
To ensure that every candidate's resume is evaluated consistently, accurately, and credibly before screening or submission.
This framework combines human judgment and AI-driven rule checks to identify authentic, experienced, and in-depth candidates.
Structure
🟢 Core 9 Categories (Total: 66 Rules) – Mandatory evaluation dimensions for all profiles.
🟡 Optional 3 Categories (Total: 14 Rules) – Contextual checks depending on role, JD, or domain.
Total Rules: 80 (52 Mandatory / 15 Optional / 13 Positive Boosts)
Legend

🟢 CORE 9 CATEGORIES (66 RULES)

1. Experience Timeline Integrity (8 Rules)
Validate chronology, stability, and authenticity of employment.
❌ Claimed vs. calculated experience mismatch > 1.5 years.
❌ Overlapping full-time roles (unless dual payroll proof).
❌ Backward or invalid dates (e.g., Feb 2018 – Jan 2017).
❌ Unexplained gaps > 6 months.
⚠️ Multiple short stints (< 12 months each) within one year → possible role inflation.
⚠️ Too-clean transitions (12M/24M/36M) → artificial smoothing.
⚠️ < 1 month gap between education & first job (unless campus placement).
✅ Stable tenure (2–4 years) → credibility boost.

2. Must-Have Skills Depth (7 Rules)
Assess depth, authenticity, and continuity of key technical skills.
❌ Depth < 3 (“applied in project”) on any must-have skill.
❌ Skill only name-dropped (score = 1) with no project context.
⚠️ Used without clarity (score = 2) → probe further.
✅ Depth 3 = Applied in project with context/tools.
✅ Depth 4 = Architected / mentored / optimised (ownership).
✅ At least one must-have skill should reach depth 4 for senior roles (8Y+).
❌ Inconsistent skill claims across roles.

3. Project Ownership & Realism (7 Rules)
Measure real contribution, ownership, and measurable impact.
❌ Missing ownership in ≥ 2 major projects.
❌ No measurable KPIs or outcomes.
❌ No team or scope context (scale, team size).
❌ Inflated ownership (“architected enterprise platform” in 6M role).
⚠️ Overly generic bullets repeated across projects (“responsible for X”).
✅ Clear personal accountability and differentiation from team/company work.
✅ Quantified KPIs (latency ↓40%, throughput ↑3x, cost ↓20%).

4. Language & Tone Realism (8 Rules)
Detect AI-written tone, over-polished phrasing, or vague resumes.
❌ Polished but vague tone with no specifics.
❌ Entirely passive voice across roles.
❌ Obvious GPT-template phrasing (“leveraged synergies”, “orchestrated outcomes”).
⚠️ > 3 vague or buzzword-heavy statements.
⚠️ Repeated phrasing across roles (> 6 times).
❌ Inflated metrics (“95% accuracy” reused).
⚠️ Overused generic verbs (> 10x: “responsible for”, “worked on”).
✅ Balanced tone (mix of technical depth + business outcomes).

5. Credibility & Authenticity Signals (7 Rules)
Ensure employer, education, and role data are verifiable.
❌ Employer vs. client mismatch (vendor claiming client).
❌ > 2 payroll/vendor layers.
❌ Full-time job during full-time education (unless justified).
⚠️ LinkedIn missing or newly created (< 3 months).
⚠️ Fake-sounding email ID.
⚠️ Employer branding contradiction (Tier-3 vendor → FAANG claim).
✅ Clear, verifiable employers and clients.

6. Proxy / Clone Detection (7 Rules)
Identify cloned or AI-generated resumes.
❌ Resume cloned (known template).
❌ Repeated identical achievements across roles.
❌ Cloned + reused KPIs → auto-reject.
⚠️ Resume formatting too uniform.
⚠️ Phrase reuse across projects.
⚠️ AI tone shift mid-resume (half passive, half detailed).
✅ Organic variation in phrasing and project style.

7. Education & Foundational Background (6 Rules)
Validate education credibility and logical career foundation.
❌ Fake or unverifiable institution.
❌ Full-time education overlapping full-time role.
⚠️ < 1 month gap post-education → suspicious unless campus hire.
⚠️ Irrelevant degree (note but not penalize).
✅ Premium institute → credibility boost.
✅ Logical transition (education → entry → progression).

8. Leadership & Career Progression (5 Rules)
Evaluate trajectory, growth, and evidence of leadership.
❌ Flat career over 8+ years.
❌ Repeated lateral roles with no progression story.
✅ Promotion within same company (Engineer → Sr. Engineer → Lead).
✅ Scope growth: mentoring, stakeholder management, design authority.
✅ Leadership activities (hiring, mentoring, tech talks).

9. Pedigree & Macro Signals (6 Rules)
Assess overall employer quality and trajectory.
❌ Repeated downgrade in employer quality.
❌ Job-hopping across Tier-3 vendors.
✅ Employer trajectory improves (Tier-3 → Tier-2 → Tier-1).
✅ Multiple Tier-1 employers → strong signal.
✅ Premium education + Tier-1 experience → high trust.
⚠️ Mixed pedigree → neutral outcome.

🟡 OPTIONAL 3 CATEGORIES (14 RULES)

10. Tools & Certification Validation (6 Rules)
Verify authenticity, relevance, and context of certifications.
❌ Fake or irrelevant certifications.
❌ Laundry list of certifications without project link.
❌ “Pursuing” certification > 6 months old.
⚠️ Tool overload (> 15 tools in short role).
✅ OEM certifications (AWS, GCP, Azure, Oracle) → credibility boost.
✅ Tools clearly tied to project outcomes.

11. JD Alignment & Broadness (3 Rules)
Ensure resume aligns with required and optional JD expectations.
✅ Broad JD → confirm with recruiter whether to cluster or evaluate equally.
❌ Missing must-have skills → auto-reject.
⚠️ JD too generic → mark as “equal weight” evaluation.

12. Domain / Industry Relevance (3 Rules)
Check domain alignment for industry-specific roles.
❌ No domain exposure where mandated (e.g., BFSI).
⚠️ Adjacent domain only (e.g., telecom vs BFSI).
✅ Multiple domain-relevant roles → credibility boost.

🧾 Summary Insights
Strict Rejection Rules (❌)
Experience overclaims, fake education, missing must-have depth, proxy/clone templates, inflated ownership, payroll chains, irrelevant certifications, and pedigree downgrades.
Warning Rules (⚠️)
Vague tone, short stints, tool overload, buzzword overuse, adjacent domains, generic JDs, or unclear depth.
Positive Rules (✅)
Promotion within company, Tier-1 employers, stable tenure, measurable KPIs, depth-4 mastery, premium education, and OEM certifications.
