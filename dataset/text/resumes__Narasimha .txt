Project 2:
❖ Title : Multi-Channel Customer Identity Resolution
❖ Client : Global Retail & E-Commerce Enterprise
❖ Environment :Informatica MDM (Hub Console, IDD, Provisioning Tool),Informatica IDMC /
IICS (CDI, CAI, Mass Ingestion),Informatica Data Quality (IDQ),Oracle Database
❖ Role : IDMC Developer
Description:
The Multi-Channel Customer Identity Resolution project was designed to address the challenge
of inconsistent and fragmented customer information collected across a wide range of business
systems. These sources included e-commerce websites, retail POS systems, CRM applications,
loyalty management platforms, mobile apps, customer service channels, and partner systems.
The goal was to build a unified and authoritative Customer 360° view that would serve as a
single source of truth for all customer-related data within the organization.
The solution was implemented using Informatica IICS, IDMC, and MDM SaaS (C360, S360) to
consolidate scattered customer identities, harmonize data across heterogeneous schemas, and
generate clean, accurate Golden Records. Advanced fuzzy matching algorithms, survivorship
rules, identity resolution logic, and trust scoring models were configured to resolve duplicate
profiles, correct inconsistencies, and merge fragmented identities into a unified customer
record.
Using CDI and CAI, the project enabled batch and real-time integrations with both legacy and
modern cloud applications. CDQ (Cloud Data Quality) was leveraged to standardize addresses,
validate emails and phone numbers, enforce business rules, and apply global reference data.
End-to-end data pipelines were automated through taskflows to support continuous updates
and incremental changes from multiple customer touchpoints.
The refined and enriched customer data was published to downstream systems including CRM,
Marketing Automation platforms, Customer Analytics solutions, and centralized data
lakes/warehouses. This allowed business teams to run more accurate segmentation,
personalized campaigns, loyalty scoring, churn prediction, and behavior analytics.
The project enhanced the organization’s ability to understand customer relationships,
household structures, cross-channel behaviors, and lifetime value. It further strengthened
compliance with data governance, PII protection, data privacy regulations (GDPR), auditability,
and enterprise-wide data quality standards. The centralized identity resolution framework
significantly reduced operational costs, improved customer satisfaction, and supported data-
driven decision-making across marketing, sales, customer service, and digital channels.
