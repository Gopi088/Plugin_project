Job Title: Digital Marketeer
Location: Pune (Work from Office – Shivaji Nagar, Pune)
Experience: 0-2 years
Client: DoRight



About DoRight:
DoRight revolutionizes philanthropy by merging technology, collaboration, and social impact. Our AI-driven platform connects NGOs, donors, and corporates to drive sustainable, impactful change globally.

About the Role
Are you passionate about design, digital trends, and storytelling through visuals? Join DoRight to shape how we engage with changemakers online!
This is a growth-focused role - you’ll start by owning our day-to-day digital voice for doright. This role is ideal for someone with a keen design sense and proficiency in tools like Canva or Adobe, looking to kick-start their career in digital marketing. Initially focused on content creation and digital engagement, the role will evolve to include collaboration with marketing agencies to drive broader marketing initiatives.

Key Responsibilities
Social Media Content Creation & Management:
Design, plan, and execute engaging social media content that aligns with the brand’s voice and strategy.
Regularly update and manage social media channels to maintain a consistent brand presence.
Design and Creative Management:
Develop visually appealing graphics using Canva, Adobe Photoshop, and Creative Cloud tools.
Ensure all digital content maintains high standards of quality and design aesthetics.
Strategy and Feedback:
Analyze the performance of social media campaigns and adjust strategies to maximize engagement.
Conduct regular reviews of the website (https://doright.world/) and other digital assets, providing critical feedback on design and user experience for ongoing improvements.

Required Skills & Qualifications
Design Proficiency:
Fluency in Canva or Adobe tools (Photoshop, Creative Cloud).
Strong design sense with an eye for detail and creativity.
Digital Marketing:
Basic understanding of digital marketing principles and social media trends.
Ability to generate fresh ideas and convert them into engaging digital content.
Communication & Feedback:
Excellent communication skills, both written and verbal.
Ability to provide detailed, constructive feedback, particularly on design aspects.
Portfolio Requirement:
A portfolio showcasing previous digital design and social media work is mandatory. Candidates will be required to present and discuss their portfolio during the interview process.

Interview Process
Interview Type: Face-to-Face (F2F)
Mandatory Portfolio Screening: All candidates must present a portfolio demonstrating relevant skills and past work.
Home Assignment: Before the interview, candidates will review https://doright.world/ critically and provide detailed feedback on areas for improvement in design and overall user experience.
Why Join Us
At DoRight, you’re not just designing posts — you’re shaping the digital voice of a platform that’s reimagining philanthropy. Be part of a close-knit, purpose-driven team based in Pune.
