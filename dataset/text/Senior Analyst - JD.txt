Senior Analyst – QA, Requirements & Product Quality 

Experience: 5+ Years 
Department: QA & Quality Control 
Reporting To: Product / Engineering Leadership 
Company: OMS Business Digitalisation Pvt. Ltd. 

About the Role 

OMS Business Digitalisation Pvt. Ltd. is building a mission-critical B2B platform where 
accuracy, stability, and controlled change are non-negotiable. 

We are looking for a Senior Analyst who brings a strong background in Software 
Testing/QA along with hands-on experience in documentation (BRD, FRD, SRS) and 
client requirement handling. 

This is a high-ownership, leadership role that sits between clients, product, 
engineering, and QA, ensuring that what is built is clearly defined, thoroughly tested, 
properly documented, and safely released to production. 

What You’ll Do

•  Own end-to-end requirements for both existing and new product features 

•  Understand the current product behavior, including reverse-engineering 

undocumented functionality 

•  Document clear, structured, and testable artifacts: 

o  BRD, FRD, SRS 

o  User stories & acceptance criteria 

o  Functional workflows, validations, data flows, reports, and limitations 

•  Ensure all requirements are reviewed, approved, traceable, and baseline-

controlled before development starts 

2. Client & Stakeholder Interaction 

•  Act as a client-facing analyst, gathering requirements from customers and 

internal stakeholders
