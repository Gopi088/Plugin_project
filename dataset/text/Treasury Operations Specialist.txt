Job Title: Treasury Operations Specialist – BAU Support 

Experience: 7–9 Years 
Domain: Capital Markets / Investment Banking 

Role Overview 

We are looking for an experienced Treasury Operations Specialist to manage BAU 
activities within a Capital Markets / Investment Banking environment. The role focuses 
on Collateral Management, Liquidity Monitoring, Stress Testing, and Reconciliation 
processes, ensuring operational accuracy, regulatory compliance, and seamless front-
to-back treasury workflow management. 

The ideal candidate should have strong hands-on experience in collateral and liquidity 
operations within a structured banking setup. 

Key Responsibilities 

1. Treasury Operations (BAU Support) 

•  Perform day-to-day treasury and collateral operational activities. 

•  Monitor liquidity positions and portfolio exposures. 

•  Support regulatory and internal liquidity reporting. 

•  Ensure adherence to internal controls and operational SLAs. 

•  Manage high-volume BAU processes with accuracy and timeliness. 

2. Collateral & Margin Management 

•  Manage Initial Margin (IM) and Variation Margin (VM) processes. 

•  Handle ISDA / CSA-based collateral agreements and margin calls. 

•  Perform exposure calculations and collateral optimization. 

• 

Investigate and resolve collateral disputes and margin breaks. 

•  Ensure accurate collateral postings and timely settlements. 

3. Liquidity Monitoring & Stress Testing 

•  Support liquidity stress testing and scenario analysis.
